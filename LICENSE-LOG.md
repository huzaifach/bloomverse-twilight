## Pick decisions (cozy-fantasy fit)
- **merge:** pizzicato (warm acoustic string pluck) over digital/glitch clicks — cozy > synthetic.
- **tierup:** longest pizzicato jingle (PIZZI07, 1.32 s) — an arc with lift, the closest to a "magical fanfare" in the Kenney set; sax/steel/8-bit jingles were rejected as wrong vibe (jazzy/tropical/chiptune).
- **wish:** interface `confirmation_002` — a soft two-tone completion chime, not a fanfare.
- **collect:** glass crystal ping over RPG coin clinks — reads as dewdrop sparkle, not tavern money.
- **error:** shortest/dullest error thud (`error_001`) — soft, not buzzy.
- **ambient-night:** CC0 crickets loop (Wolfgang_) — the canonical night-ambient pick; looped to ~46 s to hit the 30–60 s spec.
- **lullaby:** music-box-only variant of "Forgotten Lullaby" (CC-BY, attributed) — a genuine music-box lullaby loop; the synth/hurdy-gurdy variants and a CC0-but-creepy ghost-themed loop were rejected.
